Cheesecake

Home

Description

Cheesecake is a dessert made with a soft fresh cheese, and sugar, with either eggs or cream depending if it is to be baked or not. It may have a crust or base made from crushed cookies, digestive biscuits, graham crackers, pastry, or sometimes sponge cake.

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup finely ground graham cracker crumbs
  • ¾ cup pecan sandies cookies
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 3 tablespoons white sugar
  • 8 ounces cream cheese
  • ⅓ cup white sugar
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • ½ cup heavy whipping cream, whipped
  • ½ cup sliced fresh strawberries (Optional)
